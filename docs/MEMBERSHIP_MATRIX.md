**What happens when a period ends**
- **Welcome (60 days) → Free:** nothing is deleted. References above 20 stay saved and visible; you can't add new ones while you're over 20. Menus you unlocked this month stay unlocked until the month ends.
- **Founding Member (90 days) → Free:** the same rules. No charge, ever, unless the member chooses a plan.
- **The highest benefit always wins:** Founding Member Premium > paid plan > welcome Plus > Free.
